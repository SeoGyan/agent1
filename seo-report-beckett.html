4-domain fragmentation — authority scattered across beckett.com, beckett-authentication.com, beckettmedia.com, marketplace.beckett.com3–6 months
Authentication services live on a completely separate TLD (beckett-authentication.com), magazines on beckettmedia.com, and the marketplace on a subdomain. Google treats these as different websites. Every backlink to beckett-authentication.com does not benefit beckett.com. Consolidate authentication under beckett.com/authentication and magazine content under beckett.com/media.
robots.txt returns HTTP 403 Forbidden — Googlebot cannot verify crawl directives30 min
Google requires robots.txt to be publicly accessible (2xx or 404). A 403 response is treated as a crawl error and prevents Googlebot from confirming what is and isn't allowed. Whitelist /robots.txt in Cloudflare WAF as the first fix.
Zero schema markup across all page types — no rich results on any Beckett page2–4 weeks
No Product, Offer, AggregateRating, BreadcrumbList, Dataset, Organization, or Article JSON-LD detected anywhere on beckett.com. Competitor PSA earns rich results on graded card pages. Beckett's marketplace listings, grading service pages, and news articles are all plain blue links with no SERP enhancements.
Online Price Guide fully paywalled — 5.5M+ listings invisible to Google and all AI assistants2–3 months
Beckett's Online Price Guide is the industry's most comprehensive sports card pricing database (5.5M listings, 25M+ transactions). Every record is behind a subscription wall. When a user asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Mode 'what is a BGS 9.5 LeBron James rookie worth?', the AI cannot cite Beckett — it cites Card Ladder, eBay sold listings, and SportsCardsPro which publish free crawlable data. Beckett's most defensible asset generates zero AI citations.
Cloudflare WAF blocks all non-browser user-agents including AI retrieval crawlers2 hrs
HTTP 403 Forbidden is returned to ChatGPT-User, Claude-Web, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and all AI retrieval agents (not just training crawlers). This is distinct from blocking GPTBot (training). The site is invisible to AI search surfaces. Split WAF policy: block training crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot), explicitly allow retrieval crawlers (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, Claude-Web, PerplexityBot).
